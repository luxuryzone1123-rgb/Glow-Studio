About Us

A Space Made For You

Glow Studio started with one simple idea: everyone deserves to feel good in their own skin. Our team of stylists and therapists takes time to understand what you want, then works with you to get there. No rushing, no guesswork.

From your first consultation to the final touch-up, every visit is planned around your comfort, your hair, and your skin.

  • Certified, experienced stylists and therapists
  • Clean, hygienic and relaxing environment
  • Products chosen for your skin and hair type
